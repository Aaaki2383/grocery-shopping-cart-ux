# grocery-shopping-cart-ux
Mobile grocery shopping cart UX/UI design project
# User Research

## Project
Grocery Shopping Cart Mobile App

## Problem

Grocery shoppers often add multiple products to their cart and then
struggle to review their items, change quantities, find substitutions,
and understand the final cost before checkout.

## Research Goal

Understand the problems users experience when reviewing and managing
items in a grocery shopping cart.

## Research Questions

- How do users review their grocery cart?
- How do users change item quantities?
- What information do users need before checkout?
- How do users handle unavailable products?
- What makes users abandon their cart?
- What information helps users feel confident about their order?

## Key UX Areas

- Product quantity
- Remove item
- Save for later
- Product substitutions
- Delivery options
- Promo codes
- Order subtotal
- Estimated total
- Checkout

## Target Users

People who regularly purchase groceries using a mobile shopping app.

## Design Goal

Create a simple mobile shopping cart experience that makes it easy
for users to review their groceries, make changes, understand their
total cost, and proceed to checkout.
